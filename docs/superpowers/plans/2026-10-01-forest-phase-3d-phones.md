## 3d-3 Tier 2 static backdrop
`client/src/components/join/PhoneBackdrop.jsx` (CSS/inline-SVG only: NO canvas, NO rAF, NO animation, no ring). A dark-to-orange sky gradient plus a low horizon strip along the bottom, `position: fixed; inset: 0; z-index: 0; pointer-events: none`, content stacked above. Hue per station comes from the forest world: add `phone: { skies: [13 x {top, mid, horizon}] }` to `hauntedOctober.world.js` DERIVED from the forest's own per-station sky cards in `forestScene.js`/v3 (read the `.sl` sky card gradients) rather than invented. Props: `{ world, stationIndex }`; renders nothing unless `world?.renderer === 'forest'`. Mount in `Join.jsx` for waiting + live views only when the show's theme resolves to a forest world (`RING_WORLDS[theme.id]`), station = `resolveRingSlideIndex(sorted, show.current_slide_index) % 13`. Must NOT reduce contrast of any answer control: backdrop sits behind, controls keep their own backgrounds; compute worst-case text/control contrast against the brightest backdrop pixel (horizon) and require >= 7:1 for text drawn directly on it.
