The rail

Drifting stars, a small orange nebula, and now and then a shooting star. Depth is three star layers at different speeds — no objects needed. One straight line through space, long enough to last the night, with every star placed once and never reused, so nothing can repeat. The question alternates purple and orange slide to slide.

Time

What this is. Stars, a nebula, and nothing else.

Depth without objects. Three star layers run at 0.4, 1.6 and 5 px/s. The far field almost holds still, the near field slides. That speed difference is the entire parallax system — the oldest and most reliable depth cue there is, and it needs no planets, rocks or rings.

Nothing repeats, because nothing is reused. A show is finite, so each layer is one strip long enough to cover the whole night, with every star placed once by a seeded hash. No tile period, no wrap, no seam, nothing that comes back around. That entire class of bug stops existing.

No jolt, by construction. Drift runs on .rail and the advance runs on .surge, and they nest. There is no settle step and no re-basing, so there is no moment where two numbers have to agree — which is exactly what produced the pop in the previous version.

Nothing here can make a horizon. The previous revision used linear colour bands, whose edges landed near-horizontal and read as ground. Bands are gone. All colour is radial and cornered, so no straight edge can form.
