Deep Space

First artifact of the post-ambient direction. Built to the think-tank numbers, not to adjectives — five slots (void · far · anchor · drifter · occluder), mutually-prime periods (13/17/19/23/29/37s, so no two layers ever re-align inside a 2.5-hour show), three panes on a wall-clock loop, and one jittered event channel that carries comets, GIFs and sound bites as interchangeable payloads. Transform and opacity only. No requestAnimationFrame anywhere.

Slots in frame

5 — void, far, anchor, drifter, occluder. Every pane has a nameable anchor.

Periods

13·17·19·23·29·37s + drift at 88·104·127·310·430·1560s. Nothing re-aligns.

Loop

138s — 3 panes, ~40s flat hold each, 4s glides. Wall-clock phased.

Events

Ambient comet 1/40–120s jittered ±40%. GIF + sound bite 3–4/night, host-fired.

What to look at, and what it's proving

Watch it from across the room, not at your desk. The whole calibration is a 55" panel at 15 feet. On a laptop at arm's length the drift will look too slow and the grain too strong — that's correct, not a defect.

  • The period law is the finding. Layers at 10/20/30s re-align every 30 seconds and read as a loop. These are mutually prime, so the composite never repeats inside a show. It costs nothing — no extra art, no extra GPU — and it's the single biggest difference between "alive" and "a gif."
  • Every pane has an anchor. Nebula bloom, ringed planet, moon rim. This is the correction to the original six-pane idea: near-still panes with nothing to name are the repo's own definition of bland, and six of them is 6× bland. The anchor is what makes a pane a place.
  • The occluder is doing the depth work. The dark rocks clipped by the bottom corners are why it reads as space rather than a wallpaper. Toggle nothing — just notice that pane C, which has no occluder, feels flatter. That's the slot earning its place.
  • Transitions are meant to go unnoticed. Flat-hold keyframes mean each pane genuinely sits still for ~40s, then glides 4s. If you can't tell it happened, that's the design working.
  • The scrim is a band, not a wash. Full-frame scrims flatten the art to mush. This one only darkens the text band, so the anchors stay bright at the edges.

The reskin surface is five hex values plus six per-world items — palette, far texture, anchor form, drifter sprite, occluder silhouette, and horizon/light direction. Everything else on this page is shared engine. Halloween changes exactly those six and nothing else. If it needs a seventh, the engine changes for every world, not just that one.

Salvaged from the old ambient agent

Those failures produced real findings. These carry forward into the house style — the rest of that apparatus existed to police hand-authored figurative scenes, which this system doesn't have.

  • Classify before you code. One sentence of pure geometry gets hand-coded; anything identified by contour or joints gets generated. The oak failed 3 hand-coded attempts and 1 Recraft pass fixed it. The pond failed 6 non-converging ways and 1 generated texture fixed it. This world is 100% geometry on purpose — that's why it's world #1.
  • Radial-gradient margin math, computed not eyeballed. A gradient's % radii are relative to its own box. If center ± radius lands outside the box on a side that needs to taper, the box edge cuts it before it reaches transparent and you get a lit rectangle. Aim for 5–15% real margin. Every anchor here was sized against this.
  • The blur floor is a minimum, not a target. One uniform blur across the whole frame reads as "out of focus," not as depth. Graduate it — near ~1.6–2.4px, far ~3.4–4.5px.
  • A position-only check cannot verify subtle motion. The meadow swing passed 27/27 numeric checks while reading as motionless. That matters more here than it ever did: the entire aesthetic is sub-threshold drift, so any assertion has to sample the real transform across a full period, not endpoints.
  • Scan the zone's pixels, not the elements. assert-safe-zone-luminance.mjs caught three bright things nobody had named, because it scans every pixel instead of checking opacities it was told about. Extend it to sample during a pane glide — a static render can't see the one new state this design adds.
  • Never background a visual build. The done-gate doesn't reliably fire for backgrounded dispatches, so a scene can ship with zero checks having run anywhere.
  • Eased CSS animations re-apply their timing function per keyframe segment, not once across the timeline — so two properties keyed at different percentages stop sharing a progress value. linear is exempt, which is why the pane cycle is linear.

Dropped: the absolute blur floor, the in-family colour rule, and "no ground plane." The two best-reading themes already shipped — halloween and sonora-balloons — break all three. The replacement law is value separation, one hard silhouette tier at an extreme value, and no two animated things sharing a period.
