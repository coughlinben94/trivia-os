Haunted forest v4: a long woodland walk, barns and shacks

Prototype. You wander through dark woods without stopping; Next quickens the walk to the next station, then it eases back to a stroll. Each show draws its own lap (?seed=N, ?stations=N 8..80, default 36) from a pool of woodland stations: pine stands, birch groves, an old orchard, a creek with a plank bridge, a moonlit glade, deer, owls, crows, a few eerie human traces, and a barn or a shack to walk through every 7 or 8 stations; same seed, same lap, every reload. CSS transforms and opacity only, chained timers, no animation loop. ?dur= ms for a 6 m quicken, ?wander= m/s stroll pace (0.42), ?bare for a clean 1920x1080 frame. Back or a click on the strip is a covered cut.
