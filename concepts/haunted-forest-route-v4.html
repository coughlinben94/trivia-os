Haunted route v4: turns and a barn you walk through

Prototype. You wander through without stopping; Next quickens the walk to the next stretch, then it eases back to a stroll. A seeded route (?seed=N) picks each of the 13 stretches: straight, bend left, bend right, or a barn you walk into and out of; same seed, same route, every reload. CSS transforms and opacity only, chained timers, no animation loop. ?dur= ms for a 6 m quicken, ?wander= m/s stroll pace (0.42), ?step= metres per stretch, ?bare for a clean 1920x1080 frame. Back or a click on the strip is a covered cut.
