Firefly Summer — meadow ambient, continuous

A nighttime scene: a small porch and house frame the left edge. A mason jar sits on the porch rail, waiting to be filled. A treeline marks the far horizon. Between porch and treeline: an open meadow that slopes gently down to a still lake. As the scene loops, fireflies drift and pulse across the meadow and shoreline, moving slowly enough to read as truly alive rather than darting insects — each on its own path, phase, and fade cycle so no two blink in sync. The jar remains a secondary focal point (not the center safe-area), ready to become interactive in later themes.
