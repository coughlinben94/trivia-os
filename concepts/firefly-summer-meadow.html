Swing/branch: seven Fable attempts, still unresolved

Two Fable checkpoints were run per the brief, checkpoint A (oak+branch+swing) re-run seven times as each fix traded one failure for another: (1) hue-matched branch color alone — read as a floating gantry, no rope contact. (2) Thicker visible ropes + backrest removed + recolored rim/shoreline — read as scaffolding, ropes read as posts. (3, user-approved) rope twist-texture — read as a segmented rod. (4, user-approved) edge blur to match the scene's soft rendering — no perceptible change. (5, user-approved) organic curved branch/seat geometry — over-rounded seat read as an inflatable pontoon. (6, user-approved) dialed-back rounding + recolored seat to warm wood-brown (was an off-palette teal the whole time) — read as a "daylight-lit shelf," inconsistent with the scene's night silhouettes. (7, user-approved) full silhouette treatment (no slats/rim-light, oak's own verbatim sampled color) — still read as a "featureless slab," and the complaint flipped to wanting the glow/bloom treatment the pond and jar have. That reversal (too detailed → too plain) is why this stopped at seven: the target was moving, not converging. Shipped as the flat near-black silhouette from attempt 7 (27/27 numeric spec met) with this documented as a known, unresolved perceptual issue — see the self-gate below.

Pond checkpoint B findings (2026-07-26)

Checkpoint B (full frame, after pond+hills) found the water read as "ice, fog, or a spotlight pool" — too light-valued, zero visible warm reflection despite the jar glowing at its edge, and a hard uniform-rim ellipse competing with the oak's own grounding-shadow ellipse nearby. One fix pass: water fill replaced with a radial gradient (warm undertone facing the jar, cooler/darker elsewhere), shimmer highlight toned down (was reading as glare), reflection opacity raised on the oak/firefly reflections. Not independently re-verified by a second Fable pass — the improvement is judged by eye, not machine-checked the way the geometry is.
