Firefly Summer — meadow ambient, continuous

A nighttime scene: a small porch and house frame the left edge. A mason jar sits on the ground beside the porch post, already home to a few fireflies of its own. A treeline marks the far horizon. Between porch and treeline: an open meadow that slopes gently down to a still lake. As the scene loops, fireflies drift and pulse across the meadow and shoreline, moving slowly enough to read as truly alive rather than darting insects — each on its own path, phase, and fade cycle so no two blink in sync. The jar is the theme's real focal element (not the center safe-area), glowing softly with its own captive fireflies on an independent rhythm from the meadow.
Sky, stars, 3-layer treeline with oak landmark, lake, 40 fireflies (hero+filler, flash+J-stroke+drift+fade, lake reflections), the porch backdrop, and the mason jar (glow + 3 in-jar fireflies) are all built and committed (Tasks 1–6). Swing bench is still to come (Task 7).
