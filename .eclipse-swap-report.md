## 1. Noun test — self-assessment, honestly

Frozen pass criterion, written before the first render:
**PASS = a fresh viewer names this as an eclipse — a black disc with a thin bright rim of light and a
few short streamers — not a ringed planet, a sun, or a UFO.**

| attempt | what rendered | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (`attempt-1/`) | thin grey-white ring, bead at 1 o'clock, corona so faint the hole was the same purple as the sky beside it; streamers invisible at frame scale | **strike one** — read as a drawn ring / halo, not a dark disc |
| 2 (`attempt-2/`) | corona ~2x, wider blurred band hugging the rim, streamers 2x — hole now reads dark by contrast, bead sells "diamond ring" | pass on my read; still corona-first, still additive |
| 3 (`attempt-3/`, shipped) | same anatomy; rim is now a bead-to-antipode gradient (white at the bead, dimmer opposite), corona stops dialed back, one streamer rotated off the safe box | pass on my read, and — to my eye — a *better* eclipse than 2: the lopsided "diamond ring" is what the real thing looks like |

Attempt 3 was **not a third noun attempt** — attempt 2 had already passed the noun read. Attempt 3
is the safe-box luminance fix (§4 below), made in a way that keeps the read. The two-strike escalation
(closer Recraft roto-trace) was **not** triggered: one strike, then a pass. No Recraft call was made;
the research doc's existing reference reading ("dark disc + thin bright annulus + short flares") was
enough for an iconic-class object.

Skepticism note, per `ring-world-mistakes.md` rule zero: this is *my* read of *my* object, on a
1920x1080 headless frame, not a guest at the taproom distance. What I'm confident of: it is not a
ringed planet (no body, no tilt, no terminator) and not a sun (no bright core). What I'm not: whether
the hole — which is *sky*, with far/near-layer stars visible through it — reads as "black moon" or as
"empty ring" at 20 ft. That's the one thing only the TV test answers.

Anatomy, `client/src/lib/ringPrimitives.js` `kind === 'eclipse'` (~lines 1959-2110):
- `.d-glow` corona: radial **donut** — transparent to 58% of closest-side, peak at 61%, long tail.
  Nothing paints inside R; the disc is the sky through the hole. Not `makeOccluder`, nothing
  subtracted, so spec §7.2's ban never applies.
- 3 streamers (`.ec-flare`, own class): tapered clip-path arms rooted at the annulus, angles 337/196/248,
  lengths 0.78/0.58/0.44 R — all under one R, "short" per the design doc. Constants, not `r()` draws.
- SVG: a blurred wide band (inner corona) + the crisp annulus (`ringW = max(4, D*0.022)`, ~14px at
  headline size) with a `linearGradient` stroke, + a Baily's bead (blurred halo + hard white dot) at -58°.
- No tilt (face-on circle) — deliberate separator from st0/st3's -10° ellipses.
- Static apart from the shared `.pf-breathe` container fade — nothing new for `freezeFrame()` to pin.
- Hue **300, unchanged** from the record. No reason found to move it: it sits between the sky (268)
  and the rose accent (330) and the `corona` region takes it at offset 0.

Family: `radial-mass` only (SLOTS[10] already said so; unchanged).

**Real before/after, not assumed.** Baseline run on the untouched `main` checkout
(`RING_VERIFY_SKIP_LIVE=1`, html pass only — the react-live pass needs port 5173 and would have
collided with this worktree's run; instrument ten): `regression tier: all 19 checks green (1 WARN)`,
spec tier `7/16 BELOW SPEC` with the **same seven html names** as after. So: no new spec-tier failure
from this change. The 14/31 is 7 html + 7 react-live; the react-live list is the html list plus "ink per
station", which `FAILURE-LEDGER.md` already records as react-live FAIL (2026-08-16/17 entries).

**One regression appeared and was fixed, in the open:** the first full gate run on the eclipse
(commit `821abec`, attempt 2) came back `regression tier: 2/34 FAIL` — safe-box luminance cap,
**st10 p99.5 = 85 vs cap 68**, both builds (baseline record: 31). Attributed with
`concepts/tools/safebox-hit-test.mjs` (same math as the gate): the p99.5 pixels sat at r≈177-200 from
the disc centre, i.e. the annulus + the corona's 61% stop, on the lower-left arc that slot 10's
top-right placement puts inside the safe box. Fix (commit `ecb51f4`): rim gradient bright at the bead
(out of box) and dim opposite (in box), corona stops 0.62->0.40 / 0.30->0.18, blurred band 0.48->0.30,
the 152° streamer (which pointed straight into the box) moved to 196°. Re-measured with the same tool:
**p99.5 55, mean 7.8, 0.41% of box pixels above 68** — then confirmed by the full gate above (st10
p99.5 55 in both builds). No threshold moved. Margin is 13 points, comfortable but not huge; the
"pixels above cap" count (0.41%) is close to the 0.5% rank boundary, so a future brightening of
anything at st10 will tip it.

Other st10 numbers worth having: headline ink **4.5% html / 4.3% react** (band 4-9%; record was 4.1%),
so the design doc's "thin annulus can under-ink, watch the 0.04 floor" worry did not materialise.

1. **The hole is sky, not black.** Design-doc mandated (corona-first, no occluder), and it is what
   keeps §7.2 out of the picture — but stars from other layers show through the "moon". If Ben reads
   that as wrong on the TV, the honest fix is a dark fill, which re-opens the §7.2 subtractive question
   at a 5th-quietest slot. Not something to tweak quietly.
2. **Placement is OPEN** (§11a item 4). Built at 10 because that's the only sane-checked candidate.
3. **`ring-spec.lock.json` `drawnSubject.kinds` not touched** (told not to). §11a item 1 says
   `eclipse` gets added there "once built" — it is now built. Until that happens st10 stays in the
   "10/13 glow-only" spec-tier FAIL, exactly as `record` did. Zero net change, but it's the next step.
4. **`RingAmbient.jsx` not touched** (told not to). Two comments there are now stale: ENGINE comment
   (~line 56, "the `record` station") and the stationOverride contract (~line 901, "the record is the
   station in frame, and the disco sky tint"). Comment-only.
5. **`RECORD_HUE` alias survives** for `LiveScreen.jsx`. When jukebox code is next open, rename the
   import to `ECLIPSE_HUE` and delete the alias.
6. **Design-doc §5.3 saturation numbers** were "~62 / ~28"; shipped exactly 62/28. Whether the corona
   sky now reads too quiet next to `ember` is an aesthetic call — see `attempt-3/react-st11.png`.
7. **Safe-box margin** (see §4): 13 points, 0.41% over-cap pixels. Fine today; fragile to any
   brightening at st10.
8. Two historical comments still say "record" as history (`midnightGalaxy.slots.js` header,
   `ringRecolor.test.js`'s synthetic `disco` key). Left; they describe the past accurately.
9. The `impeccable`/`emil-design-eng` "A1 reference input" rule was honoured by loading both; per
   `ring-world-mistakes.md`'s own finding only impeccable's sketchy-SVG ban applies, and this object is
   pure geometry (circles, gradient stops, three clipped arms), not a path-traced scene.
