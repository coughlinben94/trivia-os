Orbit road, three stations (throwaway prototype)

One experiment: the same loop-of-stations idea, but the camera travels forward along a debris road instead of sliding sideways, and the next landmark is visible ahead before you reach it. Nothing here is wired into the app. Everything is drawn by code from one number, the station, so a refresh or jump always lands on the same picture. Motion is transform and opacity only, no animation loop. Press Advance. Query: ?duo=electric_bloom|mint_drift|neon_garden|purple_blue|amber_rose, ?bare=1 for a clean 1920x1080 frame.
