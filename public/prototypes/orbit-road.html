Free flight, three stations (throwaway prototype)

One experiment: the same loop-of-stations idea, but the camera flies forward through open space instead of sliding sideways. No ground, no horizon: asteroids tumble past, dust and stars stream by, and the next landmark is visible ahead before you reach it. Nothing here is wired into the app. Everything is drawn by code from one number, the station, so a refresh or jump always lands on the same picture. Motion is transform and opacity only, no animation loop. Press Advance. Query: ?duo=electric_bloom|mint_drift|neon_garden|purple_blue|amber_rose, ?bare=1 for a clean 1920x1080 frame.
